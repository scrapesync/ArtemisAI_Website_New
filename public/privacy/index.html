When you connect a page

Artemis works by reading the social pages you connect to it. You connect a page through the platform's own permission screen, which means we never see or store your password, and you can withdraw our access from the platform at any time without asking us.

Once a page is connected, we read:

  • Your posts, and the engagement figures the platform reports for them.
  • Comments and messages on those posts.
  • Up to two years of your page's history, so the comparisons Artemis draws are against your own audience rather than an industry average.

We use that content to do four things, and nothing else:

  • Answer your questions about your page.
  • Draft replies for you to approve.
  • Suggest when to post.
  • Estimate how a draft is likely to perform.

Four things we commit to

  • Your page data is used to answer questions about your page and nothing else.
  • It is never pooled with other accounts.
  • It is never used to train shared models.
  • You can delete it at any time, and we will action that within 30 days.

For the content on a page you connect, we act as a processor on your behalf. You decide what is on your page and why; we handle it under your instructions. For your account details and your email address, we are the controller.

People who comment on your page

When Artemis reads a connected page, it necessarily reads comments and messages written by other people. Those people are not our customers and have no direct relationship with us. We handle that content only to do the four things listed above, we do not use it to build profiles of those people, and we do not use it to contact them.

If you are a member of the public and believe a business is using Artemis to handle something you wrote, contact that business first, because they decide what happens on their page. You are also welcome to contact us at hello@artemisai.co.uk and we will help.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete it.
  • Stop or restrict how we use it.
  • Send it to you, or to someone else, in a portable format.
  • Stop using it where we have relied on a legitimate interest.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. That does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

Email hello@artemisai.co.uk and we will respond within one month. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, by phone on 0303 123 1113, or by post to Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF. We would rather you came to us first so we can put it right.

Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. The only thing we store in your browser is your choice of light or dark mode, kept in local storage so the site looks the same when you come back. It never leaves your device and it tells us nothing about you. That is why you are not being asked to dismiss a cookie banner.

Keeping data safe

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited to the people who need it to do their job, and access to connected pages runs through the platform's own permission system rather than through stored passwords. No system is perfect, and if there is ever a breach that puts your rights at risk we will tell you and the ICO within the timescales the law sets.
